Privacy Policy for pKaTool

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Your scientific data never leaves your device. The titratable group systems, pKa values, interaction energies, and pH scan settings you create in pKaTool are stored locally on your device only. They are never sent to us, to any third party, or to any server. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. pKaTool does not require an account.

Ads

pKaTool shows occasional, non-personalized banner ads, served by Google's Mobile Ads SDK, to support development. You can remove ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

This SDK, and its consent (UMP) component for the EEA/UK, collects a limited set of technical data to deliver and measure ads and meet legal consent requirements — things like a device advertising identifier, approximate location (from IP address), basic diagnostics, and which ads were shown or interacted with. Google discloses exactly what it collects, and how each item is classified, in its own published data-collection disclosure and privacy policy — see Data Google's ad SDK collects and Google's privacy policy. We link there rather than re-listing it here so this page can't go out of date if Google's own disclosure changes.

Of that data, only the device advertising identifier is currently classified as being used to track you across other companies' apps and websites — which is exactly why pKaTool asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission on first launch. Whichever you choose, ads stay non-personalized either way; declining doesn't remove or degrade anything.

If you purchase Remove Ads, the ad SDK stops making any network requests entirely — no ads, no ad-related network activity of any kind.

Your privacy choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: change your answer any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → pKaTool.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase (Settings tab inside the app) stops all ad-related network activity — no SDK requests of any kind, personalized or not.

In-app purchase

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. pKaTool never sees or stores your payment information.

pKaTool does not use cookies and does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, beyond what's described above for ads.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
