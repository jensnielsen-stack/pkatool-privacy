Privacy Policy for pKaTool

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Your scientific data never leaves your device. The titratable group systems, pKa values, interaction energies, and pH scan settings you create in pKaTool are stored locally on your device only. They are never sent to us, to any third party, or to any server. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. pKaTool does not require an account and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Ads

pKaTool shows occasional, non-personalized banner ads, served by Google's Mobile Ads SDK, to support development. You can remove ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

Because ads are non-personalized, pKaTool does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes. Google's Mobile Ads SDK and its consent (UMP) component may still process a limited amount of technical data to serve and measure ads and to meet legal consent requirements in the EEA/UK — including an IP address (used to estimate general location and to show the correct regional consent form), a device identifier, basic diagnostic/crash data, and which ads were shown or interacted with. This data is used only for ad serving, ad measurement, and SDK diagnostics, is not linked to your identity by pKaTool, and is not sold. See Google's own privacy policy for how it handles this data.

If you purchase Remove Ads, the ad SDK stops making any network requests entirely — no ads, no ad-related network activity of any kind.

In-app purchase

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. pKaTool never sees or stores your payment information.

pKaTool does not use cookies and does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, beyond what's described above for ads.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
