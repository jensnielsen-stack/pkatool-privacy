Privacy Policy for pKaTool

Last updated: August 16, 2026

pKaTool does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All data you create in the app — titratable group systems, pKa values, interaction energies, and pH scan settings — is stored locally on your device only, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent to us, to any third party, or to any server. pKaTool has no network access, uses no analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not use third-party SDKs.

If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted with it.

pKaTool does not use cookies, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not require you to create an account.

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds a new feature that involves data collection), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
