pKaTool Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests

pKaTool is a titration-curve exploration tool for modeling how titratable groups (acids and bases) behave in coupled systems, such as enzyme active sites.

If you run into a problem, have a question about how to use the app, or want to suggest a feature, email:

jens.nielsen@gmail.com

When reporting a bug, it helps to include:

  • What you were doing when the issue occurred
  • What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened
  • Your iOS version and device model, if relevant
